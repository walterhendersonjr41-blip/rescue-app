These terms cover your use of Last Chance Rescue (the "App"), operated by Walt Henderson Jr (the operating business will be a Georgia LLC once it's formed). If you use the App, you agree to these terms.

## 1. What the App does
The App lets a person who is about to lose property (a storage unit headed to lien sale, a pawn ticket, a car facing repo or impound, or similar) list it at one fixed price. A buyer pays that price, the bill owed to the facility, pawn shop, or lender is paid at the counter, and the seller keeps the surplus. The App is a marketplace. It does not own, inspect, store, or guarantee any item.

## 2. Free during launch (current)
- **The App is completely free during launch.** No fee is charged to post, browse, buy, contact a seller, or get a bill of sale.
- **No money moves through the App.** At the meetup the buyer pays the store the bill directly, and pays the seller the rest of the price directly. The seller keeps the full surplus.
- The seller's phone number is shown to the buyer when the buyer taps Buy. The buyer contacts the seller. The operator never takes calls.
- **The App and its operator never pay anyone's bills.**
- **Founding members:** the first 500 users are founding members. When fees start, they will be posted in the App and in updated terms **before** they apply. Fees will never be charged on a deal made before the change.

## 2a. Planned fees after launch (not active)
When turned on, the buyer pays a platform fee **on top of** the listing price, and the seller still keeps the full surplus. Lockers and other listings: the greater of $25 or 5% of the bill. Pawn tickets and cars: 5% of the bill plus 10% of the seller's surplus. A different option is a closing fee of the greater of $25 or 5% of the bill, charged only when a deal closes. Under either option, no-shows and unsold listings are never charged.

## 3. The counter
Both parties check the FINAL SALE box and tap "Pay cashier" when the bill is paid at the store, then both tap "Deal complete" and sign the bill of sale.

## 4. FINAL SALE and as-is
- Before tapping "Pay cashier", each party must check a FINAL SALE box. **Once the cashier is paid, there is no going back.**
- Items are sold **as-is**. Storage units are usually sold **sight unseen**, based on the seller's photos and contents list.
- Changing your mind is not a reason for a refund.

## 5. Declines, no-shows, and refunds
- **During free launch, the App collects no money, so there is nothing to refund.** Nobody is charged for a decline, a no-show, a store that won't take payment, or an unsold listing.
- **Decline before the cashier is paid:** the buyer can walk away. The listing goes back up.
- **Seller no-show:** if the seller doesn't show by the meetup time, or nobody taps "Pay cashier" within 48 hours of the deal being confirmed, the deal is cancelled, the listing is removed, and the seller gets a strike.
- **Store won't take payment:** the deal is cancelled and the listing goes back up.
- **Seller lied:** after the cashier is paid, the sale is final. The only remedy is for proven seller misrepresentation (the photos or contents list were clearly false, with photo proof), and it comes **only from the seller's surplus**, between buyer and seller. The bill paid to the store is final. The operator never pays it back.
- *(Paid mode, later: held funds, price plus fee, are refunded in full in each of the cancel cases above.)*

## 6. Seller rules, strikes, and bans
- Sellers must be the renter, pawner, debtor, or owner of record, and must upload real proof (lien/auction notice, pawn ticket, or repo/impound notice).
- Anyone can report a fake listing. The operator may remove listings and **ban sellers**. Banned sellers' listings are hidden, and they can't post.
- **Two strikes (for example, two no-shows) automatically suspend a seller.**

## 7. Cars
The title is transferred at the county tag office after the lender or impound lot is paid. Buyer and seller are responsible for the title, tax, and tag requirements.

## 8. Electronic signatures and records
Both parties sign the bill of sale electronically by drawing a signature and typing their full legal name. You agree that these signatures are legally binding under the federal ESIGN Act and the Uniform Electronic Transactions Act as adopted in Georgia (O.C.G.A. § 10-12-1 et seq.) and Tennessee.

## 9. Minimal data
We collect as little as possible: name, phone number, proof document, photos, signatures, and transaction records. We don't collect ID uploads. Phone numbers are shared only between the buyer and seller of a deal. During free launch the App takes no payment information at all. Later, any card data will be handled by the payment processor (Stripe), not the App.

## 10. No warranty; limit of liability
The App is provided "as is". To the extent the law allows, the operator's total liability for any deal is limited to the platform fee paid on that deal, which is $0 during free launch.
