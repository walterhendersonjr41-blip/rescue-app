Don't lose everything at the auction.

About to lose your stuff to a storage facility, pawn shop, lender, or impound lot (storage lien, pawn forfeit, car repo / impound)? Post it at one fixed fair price you set, with your phone number and proof. No bidding. First buyer at that price gets it. The bill gets paid in full, and the surplus goes back to you. Money sits in a hold until you both tap “Deal complete” at the counter.

Everybody wins before the sale

🏠 Owners & renters

Instead of losing it all, get your debt paid off and keep the surplus: every dollar above what's owed goes back to you. Works for storage units, pawn tickets, and cars. You set one fixed price — no bidding, no haggling.

🔎 Buyers

Get first look at units, pawned items, and cars before auction or forfeit. Pay the posted price — first come, first served. No bidding wars.

🏢 Facilities, pawn shops & lenders

Get full price: back rent, loan + interest, or tow/repo bill paid in full, often more than an auction brings. Surplus goes to the owner, not a loss on your books.

Fixed Fair Price Rule: No bidding. Each listing has ONE price set by the owner, and it must be ≥ the bill owed + the platform fee (). Everything above that is the owner's surplus. No lowball, no "tank of gas" deals.
No unlock fees. Walt never takes calls. The seller posts their own phone number and uploads proof (past-due notice, pawn ticket, or repo / impound notice with their name). The buyer sees the phone after tapping Buy and calls the seller directly.
How a deal goes: 1) Buyer pays the full price into the app hold. 2) Buyer and seller meet at the facility / pawn shop / lender or impound lot; the buyer inspects first (Decline = full refund, no fees), then both tap Pay cashier and the bill is paid in full from the hold. 3) Buyer gets the stuff, both tap Deal complete: surplus goes to the seller, platform fee to Walt, seller signs a bill of sale.
⚡ Power Play (buyers): when the buyer taps Deal complete, they earn a Power Play — first dibs on new listings. Every new listing is tagged “⚡ Power Play early access” for its first 24 hours, and only buyers with a credit can tap Buy during that window (one credit per purchase). Buyers enter their phone number when they tap Buy so the credit follows them.
Storage — on the auction block only: Only lockers with a lien sale notice can be listed. The facility lock stays on until the bill is paid, so contents can't be removed. Sellers must upload the lien / auction notice as proof and give the auction / lien sale date (must be in the future). The renter pays the lien in their own name (with buyer funds), then signs a dated bill of sale with ID.
Pawn ticket: the pawner redeems the item in their own name using buyer funds (or the shop allows a ticket transfer, if its policy and state law permit), then signs a dated bill of sale. GA and TN pawn laws vary, and shop policy applies.
🚫 FINAL SALE — no going back once the cashier is paid. Before each person's Pay cashier button works, they must check a FINAL SALE box. Items you can inspect: “I have looked over the item and accept it as-is.” Storage lockers (overlocked, sight unseen): “I accept this locker as-is, sight unseen, based on the posted photos and contents list.” The only after-sale remedy is Report seller misrepresentation — when the photos or contents list were clearly false — and any refund comes only from the seller's surplus, never from the facility, the shop, the county, or Walt.
Property: all money moves through a licensed closing attorney / escrow — never hand-to-hand.
